About Us

The Owner

How we started

Claws began as a home-based side project, hand-making press-on sets for friends who wanted salon-style nails between appointments or instead of them. What started on a small desk with a few polishes and a lot of late nights quickly turned into something bigger. Word spread through Instagram and WhatsApp, and Claws grew into a small business built entirely on repeat customers and referrals Every set is still hand-painted and finished in small batches, so quality stays consistent as the business grows. Each design is sculpted, painted, and glossed by hand from the crisp French lines to the 3D flowers and zebra prints no stickers, no shortcuts. Claws is for the baddie on the go, the soft girl who loves detail, the busy mom, the student, the bride. Salon quality without the salon appointment, reusable, affordable. As the business continues to grow, Claws hopes to reach more customers across South Africa and build a trusted brand known for creativity, quality, affordability, and excellent customer service.

Our mission

To make stylish, well-made nails accessible to anyone whether that means a ready-made set delivered in days, or a fully custom design built around your hands and your budget. We believe reusable nails are better for your wallet and your natural nails, without compromising on how they look. No damage, no waiting in a salon for hours, no chipped polish after two days. At Claws, you get salon-quality, hand-painted art that lasts, that you can re-wear, re-style, and keep in your collection. Luxury that makes sense.

What you can expect

  1. Reusable for weeks Hand-finished sets built to last through multiple wears with proper care.
  2. Made to your size Custom sets are measured and shaped to your exact nail bed, not a generic size.
  3. Small-batch quality Every set is checked by hand before it ships no factory shortcuts.
