Overview

Claws is a South African small business selling ready-made and custom press-on nails built to make quality nail styling affordable without regular salon visits. Customers can choose reusable, salon-quality sets or request fully custom designs. The brand currently operates through Instagram DMs and WhatsApp and needs a professional website to showcase products, take orders, and build customer trust. Target audience: budget-conscious, style-focused customers aged 16-35 across South Africa who shop mainly on mobile.
